Your data

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /data-request/v1.html

Ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We reply within 30 days.

If you are a teacher

Signing in gives you everything already: your saved lessons and worksheets export as files, and deleting your account removes the rows from the database rather than hiding them.

If you are a parent or a student

Please ask through the school. The school is the controller for lesson data and can clear a student's board immediately. In practice the work will already have been deleted — student boards go within 24 hours of the lesson ending, and submitted work within seven days.

What happens then

  1. We acknowledge it.
  2. We check the identity of whoever is asking.
  3. We do it, and confirm, within 30 days.
  4. We record that the request was made and what we did, without recording the contents.

If you are not happy with the answer

You can complain to your national data protection authority.
