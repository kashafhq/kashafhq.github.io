About Kashaf

Kashaf means the one who uncovers. That is what teaching is, and it is what these tools are for.

Why it exists

Online whiteboards are built for teams planning projects. Ask one to draw a contour map, a resolved vector or a histogram with unequal class widths and it cannot. Teachers end up with a photograph of a paper page.

Kashaf is built the other way round: the mathematics first, and the rest of the classroom around it.

What we will not do

  • Student accounts. A first name and a code is enough, and it keeps children's data out of the system entirely.
  • Watching anything except the board. Never the camera, the microphone, the screen, the tabs or where someone is.
  • Scoring behaviour. Discipline records written by software help nobody.
  • Advertising, analytics or trackers of any kind.

These are in the privacy notice and the page for schools, in writing.
