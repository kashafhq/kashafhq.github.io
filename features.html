What is in it

Two tools that work on their own, and a classroom layer that ties them together.

The whiteboard

Writing mathematics

  • A visual equation editor — click a template and fill in the boxes. Tab moves between them.
  • Fractions, powers, roots, integrals, limits, derivatives, brackets, accents, functions, Greek letters and symbols.
  • Matrices to 30 by 30, edited like a table with the arrow keys.
  • A maths keyboard on tablets and phones.
  • LaTeX underneath if you prefer it, hidden by default.

Drawing

  • Pen, highlighter, eraser, text.
  • Lines, arrows and smooth curves through points you tap.
  • Geometry — rectangles, ellipses, triangles, pentagons, stars.
  • Flowchart shapes — decision, data, preparation, document, database, start and end, process step, block arrow.
  • Solid, dashed or dotted, in any colour and thickness.

Graphing in two dimensions

  • Curves as y = f(x), implicit, polar or parametric.
  • Lines by gradient and intercept, by equation, or through two points.
  • Vectors by components or by magnitude and angle.
  • Resolving a vector into components, with the working printed.
  • Parallel and perpendicular vectors, with the marks and the dot-product check.
  • Intersections found and labelled with their coordinates.
  • Equations written on the graph beside the curve they belong to.

Graphing in three dimensions

  • Surfaces, drawn on the board as ink you can write over.
  • Ellipsoids, spheres, tori, hyperboloids, cones, paraboloids, cylinders.
  • Cylindrical surfaces swept out by a moving line, with the sweep animated.
  • Planes, 3D vectors with cross products and projections, space curves.
  • Traces, level curves, contour maps with matched labels.
  • Critical points classified as maximum, minimum or saddle.
  • Turn it with a finger. It is part of the board, so it saves and prints.

Data

  • Bar, horizontal bar, line, pie, doughnut, scatter, histogram, box plot.
  • Stem and leaf, with empty stems kept and a key printed underneath.
  • Grouped data — class boundaries worked out, midpoints, frequency density when the class widths differ.
  • Frequency polygons at the midpoints; ogives at the upper boundaries, with the quartiles read off.
  • Mean, median by interpolation, mode from the modal class, quartiles, variance and standard deviation, with the substitution shown.
  • Raw readings grouped into classes, and the frequency table put on the board.

On the board

  • Tables you type into, with rows and columns added where the cursor is.
  • PDFs opened as real documents — every page, sharp at any zoom.
  • Images placed and annotated.
  • Recording the board, with the recordings listed to play, save or delete.
  • Works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

Worksheet Studio

  • Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, limits, differentiation, integration, matrices, linear systems and graphs.
  • Multiple choice, true or false, short answer, fill in the blank, matching, comprehension.
  • A question bank you can export and reuse.
  • Your own questions, with images.
  • Your school logo on the sheet.
  • The worksheet and its answer key, both as PDF.
