Privacy notice

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /privacy/v1.html

The short version. Teachers have accounts and we keep their email, name and saved work. Students do not have accounts at all — they type a first name to join a lesson, and that is the only thing we hold about them. It is deleted when the lesson ends. Nothing on this site is loaded from another company, so nobody else sees that you were here.

Who we are

Kashaf provides a whiteboard and worksheet generator for teachers. TODO(company): legal entity and address.

What we collect, and why

If you are a teacher with an account

  • Email and name — so you can sign in and we can reach you about your account. Kept while the account exists.
  • Password — stored only as an Argon2 hash. We cannot read it.
  • Lessons and worksheets you save — so you can open them again.
  • The version of these terms you accepted, with the date and the address you accepted them from.

If you are a student in a lesson

  • The first name you type, so your teacher knows which board is yours. Nothing else.
  • What you draw on your own board, so your teacher can see it during the lesson and so you can carry on if your connection drops.

We do not ask for, and cannot store, a student's surname, email address, date of birth or password. There is no student account.

If you are just using the tools

Nothing. The whiteboard and the worksheet generator run entirely in your browser.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No camera, microphone, screen, tabs, keystrokes or location.
  • No engagement scores, no idle-time logs, no record of who was paused in a lesson.
  • No fonts, scripts or images loaded from another company's servers.

How long we keep things

Lesson sessions and student boardsDeleted 24 hours after the lesson ends
Work a student submitsDeleted after 7 days unless the teacher exports it
Teacher account and saved workDeleted 30 days after cancellation
Server logsIP addresses shortened after 7 days

These are enforced by a job that runs every night, not by a promise on this page.

Why we are allowed to

For teacher accounts, because we need to in order to give you the service you asked for. For student work during a lesson, on the school's instruction as part of teaching. Anything optional is asked for separately.

Who else sees it

Our hosting and file storage providers, listed on the sub-processors page. Nobody else. We do not sell anything to anyone and we do not train models on your work.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the data request form and we will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account really deletes the rows; it does not just hide them.

Children

Students use Kashaf through their school and never create an account. Because we hold only a first name for the length of a lesson, there is no child profile to build. See the page written for students.

If something goes wrong

If there is a breach that puts anyone at risk we will tell the affected schools without undue delay and within 72 hours of finding out.

Changes

Each version of this notice is numbered and dated. Older versions stay available. If we change something that matters, we will ask you to read it again the next time you sign in.
