Data processing agreement

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /dpa/v1.html

For schools that need a signed agreement. This is a draft to read; the signable copy is available on request.

TODO(legal): have a lawyer review this before charging money or signing with a school.

1. Roles

The school is the controller. Kashaf is the processor. Kashaf processes personal data only on the school's documented instructions.

2. What is processed

People: teachers employed by the school, and students taking part in their lessons.
Data about teachers: name, email, password hash, saved lessons.
Data about students: a first name and the work on their own board, for the length of the lesson.
Purpose: teaching and formative assessment.
Special category data: none is requested or required.

3. How long

As set out in the privacy notice: sessions 24 hours, submitted work 7 days, accounts 30 days after cancellation. Deletion is automatic.

4. Security

The measures on the security page form part of this agreement.

5. Sub-processors

Those listed on the sub-processors page. The school will be told before another is added and may object.

6. Confidentiality

Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality and has access only to what their job requires.

7. Helping the school

Kashaf will help the school answer requests from individuals, carry out impact assessments, and deal with the regulator.

8. Breaches

Kashaf will tell the school without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, with the nature of the breach, who is affected, and what is being done.

9. Audit

Kashaf will provide the information needed to show compliance and will allow an audit on reasonable notice.

10. Ending

On termination all personal data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires it to be kept. Written confirmation is provided on request.
