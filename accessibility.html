Accessibility

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /accessibility/v1.html

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. This page says honestly where we are.

What works

  • All of the site can be used with a keyboard.
  • Text contrast meets AA.
  • Headings are in a real order, and images have text alternatives.
  • The page reflows to one column on a phone with no sideways scrolling.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without losing anything.
  • Buttons in the tools are at least 44 pixels, the recommended touch size.

What does not, yet

  • Drawing on the whiteboard needs a pointing device. There is no keyboard equivalent for freehand drawing.
  • Graphs and diagrams are drawings; a screen reader cannot describe their contents.
  • The 3D view needs dragging to rotate.

We are looking at text descriptions for generated graphs, which would help most.
