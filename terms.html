The rules for using Kashaf. Plain language, no surprises.

Who may open an account

Account holders must be 18 or over. Students take part through a teacher's lesson and do not open accounts.

What you may do

Use Kashaf for teaching. Save your lessons, print them, and share them with colleagues. The tools themselves are provided as a service and are not licensed for redistribution.

What you may not do

See the acceptable use policy.

Your work is yours

Lessons and worksheets you make belong to you. We claim nothing over them, and we do not use them to train anything.

Availability

We will try to keep the service running, but we do not promise it will never be unavailable. Your lessons and worksheets export as ordinary files at any time, so your material is never dependent on us being here.

If we stop

We will give notice and you will be able to export everything.

Ending it

Cancel at any time. Your account and its contents are deleted 30 days later. We can close an account that breaks the acceptable use policy.
