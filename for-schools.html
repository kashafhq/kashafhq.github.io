For schools

Everything your data protection officer will ask for, on one page.

The short version

Students do not have accounts. They type a first name and join with a code. Their work lives in memory for the length of the lesson and is then deleted. Nothing on the page is loaded from another company. There is no advertising, no analytics and no behaviour scoring.

What is collected, and for how long

What we hold about a teacherEmail, name, password (hashed with Argon2), the lessons and worksheets they save.
What we hold about a studentA first name, for the length of the lesson. Nothing else. No email, no surname, no date of birth, no password, no account.
Where student work livesIn the server's memory during the lesson. It is never written to the database.
SessionsDeleted 24 hours after the lesson ends.
Submitted workDeleted after 7 days unless the teacher exports it.
Teacher accountDeleted 30 days after cancellation.
Log filesIP addresses shortened after 7 days.
Third parties in the pageNone. No analytics, no advertising, no external fonts, no error reporting.
CookiesOne, for teacher login. No banner is needed and none is shown.
MonitoringThe student's board only. Never their camera, microphone, screen, tabs, keystrokes or location.
Behaviour recordsNone. No engagement scores, no idle logs, no record of who was paused.
Uploaded imagesLocation data is stripped from every one, on the server, always.

Roles

For a school deployment the school is the controller and Kashaf is the processor. We act on the school's instructions. The data processing agreement sets that out in a document you can sign.

Security

Argon2 password hashing, TLS throughout, rate limits on sign-in and on room codes, a content security policy that permits no third-party origin, signed short-lived links for submitted work, and daily encrypted backups. The security page has the detail.

Sub-processors

Hosting and file storage only. The list names them. There are no others, because nothing else is loaded into the page.

The standard questions

Do you use student data to train anything?

No. It is not retained long enough to be usable for anything, and we do not train models.

Can a parent ask for their child's work to be removed?

Yes, through the school, at any time. In practice it will already have been deleted — see the retention table above. Use the data request form.

Is there an audit trail of what a student did?

No, deliberately. Discipline records generated by software are a liability for the school and a harm to the student.

Can students contact each other?

No. There is no student-to-student channel of any kind. Students cannot see each other's boards.

What happens if you stop operating?

Lessons and worksheets export as ordinary files. The whiteboard also runs as a single file with no server at all, so a school that has downloaded it keeps a working copy.

Getting a copy of the paperwork

The data processing agreement, the privacy notice and the security overview are all on this site and can be printed. For anything else, get in touch.
