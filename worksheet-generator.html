Add a Custom Question — any subject

In Mathematics mode, just type expressions in plain text — e.g. x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0, sqrt(16), sin(theta), 2/3 — no $ signs needed. The worksheet will show them as proper typeset equations automatically.
Fill in the cells, then click Insert — the matrix goes into whichever question field you last clicked in. Cells can hold numbers or expressions (e.g. x^2, -3, 1/2).
Tag the question with its topic so it's easy to find and reuse later — questions saved to the Question Bank are grouped by Subject + Topic.
Example — Question: "What is the capital of France?" · Options: Paris / Berlin / Rome / Madrid · Correct: A (Paris)
Example — Statement: "The sun rises in the west." · Correct answer: False
Example — Sentence: "The powerhouse of the cell is the ____." · Answer: Mitochondria
Example — Question: "Explain why plants need sunlight." · Answer: Plants use sunlight for photosynthesis to make their food.
Example — "Photosynthesis" → "Process plants use to make food"; "Mitosis" → "Cell division process"
Example — Passage about the water cycle · Sub-question: "What causes evaporation?" · Answer: Heat from the sun.

📚 Unlike auto-generated questions, everything here is entered by you — any structure, wording, difficulty, or topic you like — so it's where the real variety comes from. Save a question once and reuse it in any future worksheet, tagged by subject and topic so it's easy to find again.
This is an offline file with no server, so Export to a file to keep your bank between sessions or share it with a colleague, then Import it back whenever you reopen this tool.
