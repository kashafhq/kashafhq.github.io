Cookies

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /cookies/v1.html

There is one cookie and it is for signing in. That is why this site has no cookie banner — a cookie that is strictly necessary for a service you asked for does not need consent.

What we do not use

  • No analytics cookies. We have no analytics.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No third-party cookies, because there are no third parties in the page.

Local storage

The whiteboard and worksheet generator keep your work in the browser's memory while you use them. Nothing is written to disk unless you save a file yourself.

If you block it

You will not be able to stay signed in. The whiteboard and worksheet generator will still work — they do not need an account.
