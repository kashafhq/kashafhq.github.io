Help

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the whiteboard or make worksheets. Only to save your work and to run a lesson with students.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Download either tool and it keeps working with nothing connected. Every library is inside the file.

How do I write an equation?

Open the equation bar and click a template. You get empty boxes to fill in. Tab moves to the next one. You do not need to know LaTeX.

How do I draw a curve?

Pick Curve from the shapes panel, tap each point, then tap Finish.

Why is there no sound in my recording?

If you opened the file straight from your phone's storage, the browser will not give the page a microphone — there is no web address for the permission to attach to. Use the hosted version, or open it on a computer.

Can students see each other's work?

No, and there is no way to turn that on.

Something is broken

Tell us, and say which device and what you were doing.
