Acceptable use

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /acceptable-use/v1.html

Short and obvious, but it needs saying.

Do not

  • Put anything on a board that would be inappropriate in a classroom.
  • Pretend to be someone else, including another teacher.
  • Share a room code outside the class it was made for.
  • Upload anything you do not have the right to use.
  • Try to get past the limits on what students can do, or attack the service.
  • Use Kashaf to harass anyone.

Teachers are responsible for their rooms

You control who is admitted, what tools they have, and what is shown to the class. Use the waiting room and lock the room once everyone is in.

What happens if this is broken

We may suspend or close the account. For anything serious we will tell the school. We are not in a position to monitor lesson content and we do not try to — teachers see their own rooms, and that is deliberate.
