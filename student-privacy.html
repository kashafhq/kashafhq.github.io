What your teacher can see

Version 1.0 · 03 September 2026 · Earlier versions stay available at /student-privacy/v1.html

This page is for students. It explains exactly what happens to what you write in a Kashaf lesson.

You do not have an account

You typed a first name and a code to get in. That is all we know about you. We do not have your email address, your surname, your birthday or a password for you. Nothing on this site can identify you outside the lesson.

What your teacher can see

  • Your board. What you write and draw, while the lesson is running. There is a note on your screen the whole time saying so.
  • Whether you are working. Whether you have written anything recently, so they know who might be stuck.
  • Anything you send them by tapping Submit.

What your teacher cannot see

  • Your camera or your microphone.
  • Your screen, or anything outside this page.
  • Your other tabs, your other apps, or what you type anywhere else.
  • Where you are.

Kashaf cannot see those things either. It is not able to.

What other students can see

Nothing. You cannot see anyone else's board and nobody can see yours. There is no way for students to message each other.

How long it is kept

Your board is deleted within a day of the lesson ending. Work you hand in is deleted after a week, unless your teacher saves it. Nothing about you is kept after that.

If your teacher shows your work to the class

They can put one board on the big screen. Your name is hidden unless they choose to show it, and that is a separate deliberate step.

If you want your work removed

Ask your teacher. They can clear your board straight away.
